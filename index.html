about

Where analysis meets interface

I'm a first-year B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering student at Roorkee Institute of Technology, working at the intersection of data analytics and web development. I like the moment a messy CSV turns into a clean story — and the moment that story becomes something people can click through.

On the analytics side, I clean and explore data with Pandas, NumPy, and Seaborn, write the SQL to back it up, and package findings into Power BI dashboards. On the development side, I build with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, connecting front-end interfaces to real databases.

Recent work spans a hackathon platform for Indian art, an end-to-end e-commerce analytics pipeline, a healthcare analytics system with ML-based readmission prediction, and a conversational AI assistant built on IBM Watson.
